# Hi, I'm Oviya Vijay 👋

**Industrial Designer | B.Des in Industrial Design, VIT Vellore**

I am a final-year Industrial Design student passionate about designing products and mobility solutions that are meaningful, efficient, and sustainable. My work focuses on understanding user behavior and transforming insights into functional, aesthetically compelling designs.

---

### 🛠️ Selected Projects

* **HOWLER** — Wolf-inspired sculptural ambient desk lamp combining jackfruit wood, plywood, acrylic, and integrated LEDs.
* **KOIKLOCK** — Koi-inspired minimalist wall clock crafted with anodized aluminum, high-gloss acrylic/enamel, and anti-reflective mineral glass.
* **VELO** — Pull-up minimalist pen stand designed to elevate small stationery items for effortless access.
* **ARKA** — Biomimicry-inspired, solar-powered agricultural pest trap with a modular, serviceable chamber.
* **Hairdryer La Ferrari** — Ergonomic hairdryer concept featuring a gearshift-inspired handle and gull-wing style packaging.
